This Privacy Policy describes how **kansoGoods** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects the business contact information you provide when browsing **kansogoods.com** or requesting our wholesale product catalogs and price lists.

## Information We Collect

Because our website is designed exclusively for B2B wholesale inquiries, we only collect information that you voluntarily submit through our inquiry forms or direct email communications, including:

* **Business Contact Details:** Your name, company or store name, business email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and shipping destination country.
* **Inquiry Preferences:** Product categories, estimated order quantities, and wholesale quotation requests.
* **Basic Technical Data:** Standard non-identifying browser and traffic analytics used strictly to maintain website performance and security.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to conduct legitimate B2B wholesale communication:

* To send requested digital catalogs, wholesale price lists, and product specifications.
* To prepare accurate FOB/DDP freight estimates and process trial or bulk orders.
* To respond to sourcing questions regarding neutral packaging, customization, or lead times.

**We never sell, rent, or trade your business contact details to third-party marketing lists.**

## Data Storage & Security

Your inquiry records are stored securely within our business email and client management systems and are accessible only to authorized team members handling your wholesale account. You may request to update or delete your contact information from our records at any time.

## Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external platforms (such as LinkedIn or shipping partners). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.
